IBGE 2022 Census Sector Index — Brazil

Provider: Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE)
Source: IBGE — 2022 Census Sector Mesh and sector aggregates.
Official product page:
https://www.ibge.gov.br/geociencias/organizacao-do-territorio/malhas-territoriais/26565-malhas-de-setores-censitarios-divisoes-intramunicipais.html
Official national source archive:
https://geoftp.ibge.gov.br/organizacao_do_territorio/malhas_territoriais/malhas_de_setores_censitarios__divisoes_intramunicipais/censo_2022/setores/shp/BR/BR_setores_CD2022.zip

The Android asset is a derived, normalized SQLite index. It preserves the
468,099 source records, census attributes used by ATX Plan, municipality
summaries, and sector envelopes in a portable SQLite table. It removes the
desktop build path, replaces SQLite STRICT and RTree virtual tables for Android
API 23 compatibility, and adds version, integrity, source, CRS, and limitation
metadata.

The immediate row input is a pinned desktop-derived index with SHA-256
fe8b789027d54de02de5fd1ddac7c77325657ee09721672008cb6227009a91a7. The
official archive is independently hash-pinned as a provenance input, but the
current transformer does not parse it directly. These hashes do not by
themselves prove the upstream row-by-row archive-to-index derivation.

The package does not contain census-sector polygon geometry. It cannot render
official sector boundaries, resolve exact point containment, or calculate
population inside a coverage contour. Sector envelopes are candidate filters,
not boundaries.

Source CRS: SIRGAS 2000 geographic (EPSG:4674).
Population field: v0001, interpreted as resident population total by census
sector for this source edition. Missing population remains NoData and is never
silently replaced with zero.

The official source archive contains no machine-readable license file. The
files are published through the public IBGE download service. Review the
applicable IBGE terms before public redistribution and retain this attribution,
the exact source URL, access date, transformations, and hashes recorded in
manifest.json.
